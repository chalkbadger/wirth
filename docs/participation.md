# Participation

Projects adopting this policy name their local destinations and requirements in
their contribution guide.

## Use in your own work

Use the project to build software, conduct research, and develop supporting
infrastructure. Choose your tools and methods. Examine behavior, test claims, and
challenge assumptions. Using the project requires no upstream contribution or
publication.

## Share inspectable feedback

Bug reports, questions, reviews, and inconclusive results are welcome. Identify the
behavior or claim, revision, what you examined or ran, and what remains unresolved.
Share the evidence you have; a finding needs no patch. You can publish findings in
your chosen venue and share a link through the project's local channels.

Keep challenges and responses separately attributable. Declining a proposed change
does not refute its evidence.

## Propose bounded changes

Follow the project's contribution guide to open an issue and agree on scope before
substantial implementation. Choose your tools and meet the receiving project's
documented contribution and acceptance requirements. These govern changes to that
project; using it elsewhere needs no upstream approval.

Changes that add ongoing maintenance identify the supported scope, how it was
checked, and who maintains it. The project owner decides scope and merges. Further
support and roadmap commitments require explicit agreement.
